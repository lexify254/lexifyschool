# Curriculum source and verification note

**Status as of 2026-10-27:** Lexify's curriculum catalogue is editable seed data, not an official KICD or KNEC determination. The product brief specifically requires a visible warning to verify current subject names, combinations, codes and grading against official documents. Keep that warning visible to administrators and avoid describing provisional rules as authoritative exam advice.

## Sources consulted

- Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development, [Lower Primary curriculum materials](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/lower-primary/). The page is a directory of official lower-primary designs. Its current HTML could not be reliably extracted during this review; use the linked Grade 1–3 design documents for a full subject-by-subject confirmation.
- Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development, [Grade Six curriculum designs](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-six-designs/). The page identifies official Grade 6 designs; its current HTML could not be reliably extracted during this review.
- Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development, [Curriculum designs directory](https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/), a directory for grade-level curriculum designs. The public page was not text-readable through extraction during the review.

Search-result excerpts from those official KICD pages indicate, among other areas, Creative Activities, CRE, English Activities, Environmental Activities, HRE, Indigenous Languages, IRE, Kiswahili and Mathematics for lower primary, and Agriculture, Arabic, Creative Arts, CRE, English, French, German, HRE, Indigenous Language, IRE, Kiswahili, Mandarin and Mathematics for Grade 6. These excerpts are not a substitute for reviewing the original design PDFs. Do not infer that every subject applies to every grade from those excerpts alone.

## Seed-data rule

The supplied master brief is the primary source for Junior School Grades 7–9, Senior School Grades 10–12 and 8-4-4 Form 3/4 defaults. For Grades 1–6, use conservative, clearly marked provisional catalog entries only where necessary, keep them admin-editable, and flag them for school/KICD verification. Do not label provisional records as KICD-approved.
